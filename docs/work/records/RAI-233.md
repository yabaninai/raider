## Resulting behavior

`Session.observe` and the live pump are coordinated by a shared delivery pointer, eliminating the observe-dedup race (RAI-231 flake): an observe batch can no longer re-deliver live frames, and the live pump can no longer re-deliver observe-batch frames. Every observation frame reaches the wire exactly once.

Mechanism (in `Session.Core`): a `delivery: Ref[Long]` holds the highest sequence admitted to the wire by either path; a `deliveryLock: Semaphore(1)` serializes admissions. `observe` filters the retention snapshot by `seq > afterSequence && seq > delivery` (empty → `observe_failed` "no undelivered observations after sequence N"; the frozen consumer tolerates `observe_failed` error frames), then uninterruptibly commits `delivery = missed.last.seq` and enqueues the batch through `outbound.receipt` (lossless, bounded by the stall watchdog). `forward` delivers under the lock uninterruptibly: `env.seq > delivery` → `outbound.observation` + pointer advance, else suppressed. Retention snapshots are taken inside the lock, so the filter and the commit are atomic against the hub's strictly increasing sequences. Cancellation cannot drop or duplicate frames: pointer updates happen before enqueue, and enqueue waits are lossless.
