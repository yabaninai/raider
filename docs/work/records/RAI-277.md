## Resulting behavior

`docs/architecture.md` section 6 no longer enumerates the built-in coder
toolset as six items ("file read, search, edit, patch, tree, and process
execution") — the stale Phase-4 wording left untouched by RAI-273 and routed
to this card. It now names the eight-tool workspace registry in the order
used by the RAI-270 `CoderBasePrompt` and the RAI-273 `docs/api.md`
"Workspace tools" table, with one summary-level line per tool:

- `fs_read` — bounded ranged read returning content and a SHA-256.
- `fs_search` — literal search with explicit truncation.
- `fs_edit` — atomic whole-file replacement keyed on an expected SHA-256.
- `fs_patch` — context-checked unified diff applied atomically to one file.
- `fs_patch_multi` — multi-file git-style diff; all-or-nothing validation
  against per-file SHA-256 provenance.
- `fs_delete` — irreversible deletion of exactly one regular file, guarded by
  the current SHA-256.
- `fs_tree` — bounded workspace tree listing.
- `proc_run` — shell-free structured argv process execution with bounded
  output.

A new sentence points per-tool argument/result semantics to the
[`api.md`](api.md) "Workspace tools" section (api.md owns the deep
semantics; architecture.md carries summaries only, per the card non-goal).
The two trust-boundary sentences that closed the old paragraph (process
permissions; tool policy does not sandbox arbitrary Scala) are preserved
verbatim, as is the rest of section 6 and every other section. The
introducer names the wiring entry point `CodingToolset.make(workspaceRoot)`,
matching api.md §Workspace tools. Documentation-only: no code, prompt,
registry, or gate-policy change; no restructuring beyond the enumeration.
