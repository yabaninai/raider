## Resulting behavior

`MultiJobExport.connect` now fires both loud guards **before** any segment side effects: the per-level anchor-unresolved die and the campaign-job-id-not-set die both happen prior to `EventsJsonlWriter.open`, `Segment` construction, and the `segmentsRef` registration. A fork whose introduction arrives before `join.campaign` commits now dies owning **nothing**: no registered orphan in `segmentsRef`, no segment directory, no 0-byte native file, no joined lines. The Terminal export sees `segments.isEmpty` and produces `Left(ReduceError.MissingTerminal(job))` — typed, honest, unrecoverable-before-retry by design.

Deliberate semantic choice (unchanged from the audited predecessor draft, disclosed): the invocation-counter bump and `pending.getAndSet` still happen before the guards. A guard-die connect therefore consumes an invocation number without delivering an envelope — loud by design (the die is visible in logs; the gap makes the lost invocation observable instead of silent). No public API or schema change.

Pre-existing test **SW-01** incidentally pinned the buggy behavior: its fixture published a child introduction without ever calling `join.campaign`, so `connect` registered the segment and then died at the campaign guard — the orphan's presence is what its `segments.exists(...)` assertion observed. Adapted minimally to the documented runner discipline (`driveCampaign`/`driveNested` precedent from RAI-226): the fixture now calls `join.campaign(JobId("j_sw01-campaign"))` before forking the child introduction. The assertion body is verbatim unchanged. Evidence that the adaptation is sufficient and not masking the product bug: SW-01 is green on the baseline product with the adapted fixture (revert probe below, 8 passed including SW-01) and green on the fixed product.
