## Resulting behavior

raider-runtime/scopes: structured scopes + subtree cancellation (runtime-contracts §5):
- `RaiderScope`: fork (launch+register is one uninterruptible operation, SCOPE-03 no-orphan; rejects forks after close begins), typed idempotent join, addFinalizer (LIFO), close(grace): cancels unjoined children with **bounded grace for the entire cancel+settle operation** (an uncancellable child cannot hold the scope hostage — it is reported as Uncertain, not fake Cancelled), runs finalizers in reverse order, and returns a truthful `ScopeCloseReport` (cancelled/uncertain/finalizerFailures/interrupted);
- `RaiderScope.scoped`: every body exit is followed by mandatory close; body domain failure is preserved; successful body + incomplete cleanup → typed RA-TOOLUNCERT (fake success is forbidden); interrupted cleanup → RA-CANCEL.

**Two real product bugs were found and fixed** (in RAI-007 code, exposed by SCOPE acceptance):
1. Job fibers were forked into the caller's scope → ZIO closing the test/workflow scope waited forever for children; now `forkDaemon` (jobs belong to JobManager).
2. `forkDaemon` inside a (nested) uninterruptible section created a child with an initially uninterruptible status → `cancel` never completed (reproducer: cancelAny 3000ms timeout, status stuck in Cancelling). Fix: `Task.zio(task).interruptible.forkDaemon`; after the fix — 8ms/2ms, terminal Cancelled. Both were reproduced with minimal debug mains before the fix.
