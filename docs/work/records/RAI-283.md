Asserts the `Delegated`/`child_id`/`await_agent` wire encoders, 2 `Succeeded` await results, and the final text `RAI-283 delegation propagation done`.
3. **Subtree cancellation settles promptly.** Child runs `/bin/sleep 120` under `timeout_s: 120`; the parent cancels via `cancel_agent` (`"cancelled":true`), awaits `RA-CANCEL`, finishes `RAI-283 cancel done`. Asserts the whole run settles in < 30 000 ms, child envelopes show `JobStatusChanged → Cancelling` and `Terminal(Cancelled)`, and the campaign finishes `Terminal(Succeeded)`. This leg exposed a genuine product gap (below) and is the card's sanctioned RED probe.
4. **Aggregate dispatch cap (RAI-169).** `--max-dispatches 2` with a third `delegate_agent` in flight: process exit 23, run.json `"exitCode":23`, summary `FAILED — RA-PBUDGET: aggregate dispatch cap of 2 reached; further model dispatch is refused`, transcript `RunFailed(RA-PBUDGET)`, campaign `Terminal(Failed)`, exactly `stand.requests.size == 2` model calls, typed `RaiderError.ProviderBudget("aggregate dispatch cap of 2 reached; further model dispatch is refused")` in `RunResult.error`.

Product fix required by leg 3: `ProcessTool.exec` waited in a single `proc.waitFor(timeout, unit)` inside one `ZIO.attemptBlocking`. `Process.waitFor` swallows `Thread.interrupt`, so a cancelled job parked in a long `proc_run` lingered until the OS process exited naturally (measured 120 814 ms for a 120 s sleep). The wait is now an interruptible slice loop (one 200 ms `waitFor` slice per `ZIO.attemptBlocking` step, deadline checked per slice), and an `.onInterrupt` finalizer owns the destroy (`destroy()` → 2 s grace → `destroyForcibly()`). Drain reader threads are daemons. Normal-path and timeout-path semantics are unchanged (graceful-first kill, declared `timedOut`, unkillable note, `CleanupGuarantee.DirectChildOnly` honesty preserved; grandchildren/process-group remains the declared RAI-001 gap).
