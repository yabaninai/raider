## Limitations / blockers

- BLOCKER (other party's code, contract-change request): `TabShellHostPtySpec`
  (frozen, RAI-102) P2W-01 flakes during full sbt-test under high CPU
  contention — there are now three PTY specs in raiderRepl. Root cause
  (established precisely from failed-run transcripts, evidence:
  artifacts/rai-105/repro-ref/p2w01-torn/): the driver sends `:quit` immediately
  after `s.waitFor("*1. alpha [", …)` (around line 360), but this substring is
  present in the transcript from any earlier pump render of the bar — the
  condition is satisfied immediately, `:quit` is sent while the feeder is
  still streaming (in torn sessions, a bar row shows badge “(189 unread)” for
  EventsPerTab=220), and the `:quit` echo gets joined to an incomplete row:
  ` 2. beta [running…] (189 unread):quit` → RowLike violation → `bad=1` when
  `bad==0` (`TabShellHostPtySpec.scala:396`). Statistics: full
  raiderRepl/test with my specs — 2/3 failures (894 rows, bad=1), without my
  PTY specs — 2/2 green, isolated testOnly — 2/2 green. This is NOT a
  threshold and NOT product code — it is ambiguous test-driver synchronization.
  Proposed micro-fix (for coordinator, one line in
  TabShellHostPtySpec.p2w01): before `s.send(":quit\r")`, wait for a unique
  marker for full delivery — `s.waitFor("(221 unread)", 30.seconds)` (final
  pump render when lastSeq==221), or clear the cleaned buffer / wait for the
  block tail (hintBar) after `:tabs`. Card RAI-105 is not allowed to edit the
  frozen file; until fixed, the full gate may require 1–2 retries.
- Exact intermediate badge values in the PTY transcript are not checked: pump
  rendering races with bumpUnread (eventually-consistent) — this property of
  the accepted RAI-061 pump is documented in the spec; exact values are checked
  by the report oracle.
- PTY transcript atomicity is evaluated on the final cleaned transcript (same
  class of limitation accepted in RAI-062/RAI-102).
- artifacts/tools/m2 (+cp-*.txt) were copied into the worktree from root
  (untracked, §1a) — required by the compile-fixtures gate; they are not added
  to git.
- docs/lineage.md §9.2 and board — outside lane allowed paths; coordinator will
  update at merge (gap: the product surface for unread badges is covered by
