## Resulting behavior

The Outbound writer can no longer exit while receipts are still admitted to the waiter list. Previously, a receipt suspended on a bytes-full queue (registered waiter) could be woken after the writer had already picked the terminal frame; its landing then raced the writer's exit, and a landing into the post-pick queue stranded the receipt unwritten (queue never drained to 0, surplus count short) — a timing-dependent loss of an accepted run receipt. Now:

- `State.waiters` is landing-tracked: entries are removed exactly when the waiter's retry enqueues, when the waiter is failed by channel death, or on interrupt; the writer's park predicate (`awaitWriterWork`) is "no queued frames AND no admitted waiters (or work available)".
- The pick function's terminal/exit cases (`WriteTerminal`, `Done`) are gated on `waiters.isEmpty`: with an admitted waiter, the writer parks instead and `wakeChannel` (single wake channel replacing per-waiter promise fanout plus the writer promise) wakes it when the last waiter lands.
- Writer wake and waiter wake no longer share one promise: the writer parks on a dedicated promise, eliminating the predecessor race where a waiter landing consumed the writer's wake signal.

Deterministic behavior at the tip: the writer drains every admitted receipt before the terminal pick in all interleavings; `writer` exits only when the queue is empty, no waiters remain, and input is closed (terminal written or none due).
